Residential units:

Located in the heart of downtown Red Deer, Swerve is located within walking distance of parks, trails, public transit, and owner-operated restaurants, pubs, boutique shops, art galleries and theatre. It’s close to health and social services, and grocery stores. You can visit your friends in the ‘burbs any time you want, but you’ll be glad to return home where everything you need is at your doorstep, overflowing with character.

Swerve contains 32 single floor suites and 4 townhouse lofts. Every unit has a balcony and commands an amazing view of the river or the parks system. Suites on floors 3 through 6 also look inward to a green courtyard.

Commercial opportunities:

The main floor of Swerve contains five spaces for food-related businesses. Imagine the ease: not only are you free of yard and vehicle maintenance, you have immediate access to unique restaurants, delis, bakeries, coffee houses and more. That gives you even more time to compose that sonata, pump iron, do the crossword, or wander the streets and trails with your camera in hand.

Businesses: it’s your dream clientele – people who’ve made a lifestyle choice, people for whom time, style, and the environment are a priority. Contact us to find out how you can get involved.
